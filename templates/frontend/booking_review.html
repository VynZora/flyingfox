Terms & Conditions

Please read these Terms & Conditions, including our Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy, carefully before confirming your booking.

1. Booking & Confirmation

All bookings are subject to availability. A booking will be considered confirmed only after successful payment and receipt of booking confirmation from FlyingFox Adventures.

  • Customers must provide accurate booking and participant information.
  • The booking date, selected ride, number of participants and allocated time slot should be verified before completing payment.
  • Booking confirmation and ticket details will be provided after successful payment.

2. Participant & Safety Requirements

Every participant must comply with the applicable age, weight, health and safety requirements of the selected adventure activity.

  • Weight restrictions applicable to individual rides must be followed.
  • Participants must follow all instructions given by FlyingFox Adventures guides and safety personnel.
  • All safety equipment provided for an activity must be used as instructed.
  • FlyingFox Adventures may refuse participation where applicable safety requirements are not satisfied.

3. Cancellation Policy

  • If a cancellation request is submitted within 24 hours from the booking confirmation, the refund will be processed after deducting applicable Payment Gateway Charges, GST/Taxes, and processing charges (if applicable).
  • If a cancellation request is submitted after 24 hours from the booking confirmation, no refund will be provided.
  • For cancellation requests submitted after 24 hours from booking confirmation, rescheduling may be allowed based on slot availability and management approval.
  • Cancellation requests submitted after the scheduled ride time will not be accepted.

4. Rescheduling Policy

  • Each booking is eligible for one-time rescheduling only.
  • Rescheduling requests must be submitted at least 24 hours before the scheduled ride time.
  • The requested new date and time will be confirmed based on available slot availability.
  • Once a booking has been rescheduled, any further cancellation will be non-refundable.
  • If the customer fails to attend the rescheduled ride, the booking will be treated as a No Show.
  • No refund will be provided for a No Show after rescheduling, and further rescheduling will not be allowed.

5. No Show, Late Arrival & Non-Participation

  • The customer's ticket will be valid for 3 hours from the booked ride time.
  • For example, if the booked ride time is 10:00 AM, the ticket will be valid until 1:00 PM.
  • If the customer does not arrive within the allowed 3-hour validity period, the booking will be considered a No Show.
  • No Show bookings are non-refundable and cannot be rescheduled.
  • If a customer arrives at the location but voluntarily chooses not to participate in the ride, no refund will be provided.
  • Customers arriving after the allowed validity period may be accommodated only based on slot availability and management approval. No refund will be applicable in such cases.

6. Cancellation by FlyingFox Adventures

FlyingFox Adventures reserves the right to cancel or postpone an adventure activity where operating the ride is considered unsafe or impractical, including due to:

  • Adverse weather conditions
  • Heavy rain
  • Strong winds or lightning
  • Technical issues
  • Safety concerns
  • Other unforeseen circumstances

In such cases, customers will be offered either one free rescheduling or a 100% refund after deducting applicable Payment Gateway Charges and processing charges.

7. Refund Processing

  • Approved refunds will be processed to the customer's original payment method.
  • Refunds generally take 5–10 business days, depending on the customer's bank or payment provider.
  • Applicable Payment Gateway Charges, GST/Taxes, and processing charges will be deducted from the refund amount.

8. Risk Acknowledgement

Adventure activities involve inherent risks. Participants are expected to understand the nature of the selected activity, follow all safety procedures and comply with instructions provided by FlyingFox Adventures staff.

9. Policy Acceptance

By completing a booking with FlyingFox Adventures, the customer confirms that they have read, understood and agreed to these Terms & Conditions and the Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy.
