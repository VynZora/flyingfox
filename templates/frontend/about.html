About Flying Fox

Experience Munnar From The Sky

Flying Fox Adventure brings a different kind of outdoor experience to the hills of Munnar. With more than 10 years of adventure experience, we give visitors the chance to see the valleys, forests and greenery from above while enjoying a professionally operated zipline ride. Our focus is simple: make adventure activities in Munnar exciting, well-organised and safe for every rider. Families, couples, friends and adventure lovers can choose from our zipline experiences and enjoy the scenery from a completely different viewpoint. Every ride is supervised by trained professionals and uses certified safety equipment. Before you take off, our team explains the activity, checks your equipment and makes sure you're ready for the ride.

Thrilling Zipline Rides

Fly above the green hills and valleys of Munnar on one of the area's most exciting outdoor experiences. Our rides are designed for visitors who want more than the usual sightseeing stops.

Certified Safety Team

Our trained instructors supervise every ride and use certified safety equipment, including helmets and secure harness systems. Pre-ride checks are carried out before participants leave the platform.
