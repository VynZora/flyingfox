About Flying Fox

Experience Munnar From The Sky

Flying Fox Adventure offers an unforgettable zipline experience across the breathtaking hills and valleys of Munnar. Combining spectacular scenery with internationally recognized safety standards, we create thrilling outdoor adventures for families, friends, couples, and adrenaline enthusiasts. Every ride is operated by trained professionals using certified equipment to ensure an exciting, safe, and memorable journey above nature.

Soar above lush forests and scenic valleys while enjoying one of Munnar's most exciting outdoor adventure experiences.
