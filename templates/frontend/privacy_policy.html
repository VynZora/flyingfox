Flying Fox Adventure respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect when you use our website and booking services, why we use it and how it may be handled.

01

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our website, we may collect different types of personal and booking information.

Account Information
  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Login and account information
Booking Information
  • Selected activity or ride
  • Booking date and time
  • Number of participants
  • Participant details
  • Booking status
  • Coupon or promotional information
  • Booking reference
Participant Information

We may collect information necessary to determine booking eligibility and safely administer an activity, including participant names, age or other information required during booking.

Payment Information

Depending on the payment service used, we may receive limited transaction information such as:

  • Transaction or reference ID
  • Payment status
  • Amount paid
  • Payment method information
Communications

If you contact us through the website, email, telephone, WhatsApp or another communication channel, we may receive the information you provide in that communication.

02

How We Use Your Information

Personal information may be used to:

  • Create and manage customer accounts
  • Process ride bookings
  • Confirm payments
  • Generate booking confirmations
  • Generate QR or digital tickets
  • Verify bookings at the activity location
  • Send booking updates
  • Provide customer support
  • Process cancellation or refund requests
  • Maintain transaction and booking records
  • Improve our website and services
  • Detect fraud, misuse or security issues
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements
03

Booking Confirmations & WhatsApp

Where customers provide a phone number and the service supports it, booking confirmations, tickets, QR codes, reminders or important booking updates may be communicated through WhatsApp or similar communication services.

Third-party messaging services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

04

Payment Processing

Online payments may be processed using third-party payment service providers.

These payment providers may collect and process payment information according to their own privacy policies and security requirements.

Customers should never send sensitive payment-card credentials through ordinary email, WhatsApp or contact forms.

05

Cookies & Technical Information

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies required for functionality, authentication, security, preferences and website analytics.

Technical information may include:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device information
  • Website activity
  • Session information
  • Date and time of access
06

How We Share Information

Flying Fox Adventure does not sell personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers where reasonably necessary for:

  • Payment processing
  • Website hosting
  • Email delivery
  • WhatsApp or messaging communication
  • Analytics
  • Technical support
  • Booking administration

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or a lawful government or court request.

07

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no website, database or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Customers should keep passwords, booking references and QR tickets secure.

08

Data Retention

Personal information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide booked services
  • Maintain accounting records
  • Maintain transaction records
  • Handle customer enquiries
  • Resolve disputes
  • Prevent fraud or misuse
  • Meet applicable legal requirements
09

Children's Information

Bookings involving minors should be completed or authorized by a parent or legal guardian where required.

We do not intentionally collect unnecessary personal information from children.

10

Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may be able to request:

  • Information regarding personal data we hold
  • Correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion of certain personal information
  • Withdrawal of consent where processing relies on consent
  • Assistance with privacy-related concerns

Certain records may need to be retained for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention or legitimate business purposes.

11

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services.

Flying Fox Adventure is not responsible for the privacy practices of independent third-party websites.

12

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, booking process, technology or legal requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
