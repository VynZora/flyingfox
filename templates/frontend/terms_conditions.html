General Information

Flying Fox Adventure provides recreational and adventure activities including Flying Fox, zipline experiences and related services.

All activities are subject to availability, weather conditions, operational requirements, safety requirements and instructions provided by our trained staff.

02

Booking & Registration

Customers may be required to create an account or provide personal information before making a booking.

You must provide accurate information including your name, phone number, email address, participant details and other information requested during the booking process.

A booking is considered confirmed only after the required booking process and payment have been successfully completed.

Participant Requirements

Participants must satisfy the eligibility and safety requirements applicable to their selected activity.

  • Minimum or maximum age restrictions
  • Minimum or maximum weight restrictions
  • Appropriate physical condition
  • Suitable clothing and footwear
  • Compliance with safety instructions
  • Correct use of required safety equipment

Requirements may vary depending on the selected ride or activity.

Flying Fox Adventure reserves the right to refuse participation where applicable safety requirements are not satisfied.

04

Safety Rules

Participant safety is our priority. Every participant must:

  • Attend the required safety briefing
  • Follow instructions given by guides and staff
  • Wear all required safety equipment
  • Use safety equipment correctly
  • Remain within designated activity areas
  • Inform staff about circumstances that may affect safe participation

Participants who intentionally ignore safety instructions may be removed from the activity.

05

Adventure Activity & Risk

Flying Fox and zipline activities are adventure activities and may involve inherent risks.

These may include heights, speed, outdoor terrain, changing weather, slips, falls, equipment-related risks and other risks normally associated with outdoor adventure activities.

Participants are responsible for following all safety instructions and correctly using the safety equipment provided.

Children & Minors

Participants under the applicable age of majority may require permission from a parent or legal guardian.

Additional age, height, weight or participation requirements may apply to individual rides.

Health & Medical Considerations

Participants should consider whether they are physically capable of safely participating in the selected activity.

Please inform our staff before participating if any circumstance may affect your ability to participate safely.

Our staff may refuse participation where there are reasonable safety concerns.
