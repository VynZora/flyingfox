Safety is an essential condition of participation in Flying Fox Adventure activities.

Participants must:

Arrival & Ticket Validity

Customers should arrive with sufficient time to complete any required verification, preparation or safety briefing.

Ticket Validity: A customer's ticket is valid for 3 hours from the booked ride time.

For example, if the booked ride time is 10:00 AM, the ticket will remain valid until 1:00 PM.

Participation during this period remains subject to operational conditions, safety requirements and applicable slot availability.

08

Cancellation Policy

Cancellation Within 24 Hours of Booking Confirmation

If a cancellation request is submitted within 24 hours from the booking confirmation, an eligible refund will be processed after deducting applicable payment gateway charges, taxes and processing charges where applicable.

Cancellation After 24 Hours of Booking Confirmation

If a cancellation request is submitted after 24 hours from booking confirmation:

  • No refund will be provided.
  • Rescheduling may be considered subject to slot availability and management approval.

Cancellation requests submitted after the scheduled ride time will not be accepted.

09

Rescheduling Policy

  • Each booking is eligible for one-time rescheduling only.
  • Rescheduling requests must be submitted at least 24 hours before the scheduled ride time.
  • The new date and time will be confirmed based on available slots.
  • Once a booking has been rescheduled, any further cancellation will be non-refundable.
  • A rescheduled booking cannot normally be rescheduled again.

If the customer does not attend the rescheduled ride, the booking will be treated as a No Show.

In that case:

  • No refund will be provided.
  • Further rescheduling will not be allowed.
10

No Show, Late Arrival & Non-Participation

If a customer does not arrive within the permitted 3-hour ticket validity period, the booking will be considered a No Show.

No Show bookings:

  • Are non-refundable.
  • Cannot be rescheduled.

If a customer arrives at the location but voluntarily chooses not to participate in the booked activity, no refund will be provided.

Customers arriving after the allowed ticket validity period may be accommodated only where slots and operational conditions allow and where management approves the request.

Accommodation after the permitted validity period does not create an entitlement to a refund.
