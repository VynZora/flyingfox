Safety First

Your Safety, Our Promise

We use internationally certified equipment and follow strict safety procedures to provide a secure and unforgettable Flying Fox adventure.

Safety Helmet

High-impact certified helmets designed to provide maximum head protection during every ride.

Full Body Harness

A secure and comfortable harness with adjustable straps for a safe and perfect fit.

Zipline Trolley

A premium-quality trolley engineered for smooth, stable and controlled movement along the cable.

Safety Carabiners

Strong double-locking carabiners provide a secure connection between the rider, harness and trolley.

Protective Gloves

Strong-grip gloves improve hand protection, comfort and control throughout the adventure.

Safety Lanyard

A durable shock-absorbing lanyard helps reduce sudden impact while maintaining a secure connection.

Certified Equipment

Internationally certified and regularly maintained safety gear.

Pre-Ride Inspection

Every piece of equipment is checked carefully before each ride.

Trained Guides

Every ride is managed by trained and experienced adventure professionals.

Double-Lock System

Advanced dual-lock connections offer additional security.

Weather Monitoring

Rides operate only when weather conditions are safe.

Emergency Response

First-aid support and emergency procedures are always ready.

How It Works

Your Adventure Starts In Just 6 Easy Steps

Register, choose your preferred Flying Fox ride, add participant details, complete the secure payment and receive your QR ticket directly on WhatsApp.

01

Create Your Account

Register using your name, mobile number and email address. Already registered users can sign in and continue booking.

Register Now
02

Choose Your Ride

Select your preferred Flying Fox ride, available date and convenient time slot.

03

Add Participant Details

Enter every rider's required information, including name, age, weight and contact details.

04

Make Secure Payment

Review your booking summary and complete the payment securely using the available online payment method.

UPI VISA Mastercard
05

Get WhatsApp Ticket

After successful payment, your booking confirmation and QR ticket will be sent directly to your WhatsApp.

06

Verify Your Ticket & Fly

Show the QR ticket at the Flying Fox counter. Our team will verify your booking, provide safety equipment and prepare you for the adventure.

Safety First Gear Up Start Riding

Your ticket includes a unique booking ID and QR code.

Keep the ticket ready on your mobile phone when you arrive at the venue.
