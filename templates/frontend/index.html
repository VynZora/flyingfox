Safety First

Your Safety, Our Promise

A good adventure starts with the right equipment and careful preparation. Flying Fox Adventure uses certified safety equipment and follows pre-ride procedures to help keep every participant properly secured.

Safety Helmet

High-impact certified helmets designed to provide dependable head protection throughout every Flying Fox ride.

Full Body Harness

Adjustable full-body harnesses help provide a secure and comfortable fit before every launch.

Zipline Trolley

Professional-grade trolley systems provide smooth and controlled travel along the Flying Fox cable.

Safety Carabiners

Strong locking carabiners securely connect the rider, harness and trolley during the adventure.

Protective Gloves

Strong-grip protective gloves improve hand comfort and protection throughout the ride.

Safety Lanyard

Durable safety lanyards maintain a secure connection between the rider and the primary zipline system.

Certified Equipment

Safety gear is regularly inspected and maintained before operation.

Pre-Ride Inspection

Equipment and connections are checked carefully before every ride.

Trained Guides

Experienced adventure staff guide riders through every stage.

Secure Connections

Multiple secure connection points provide additional rider protection.

Weather Monitoring

Operations continue only when weather conditions are suitable.

Emergency Support

First-aid and emergency procedures are available at the activity location.

Safety Instructions

Flying Fox Adventure Munnar

Please read and follow all Rope Course and Zipline safety instructions carefully before participating in any Flying Fox Adventure activity.

Participants must meet the specified weight requirements:

Normal Zipline: 15–120 kg
Superman Zipline: 30–120 kg

Please remove or securely store all loose belongings including money, jewellery, watches, mobile phones, cameras, loose-fitting shoes, bags and glasses before entering the Rope Course or Zipline.

All larger personal belongings must be left with a member of your group before participating in the attraction.

The ride operators accept no responsibility for the loss, misplacing or damage of any personal articles.

You must be safely secured in the harness. If the harness cannot be fitted correctly, you will not be permitted to participate. This decision is at the discretion of the operator.

The safety harness must only be secured and closed by the Flying Fox operator before participation.

Participants must follow all instructions and body-positioning guidance given by the operator while wearing the harness and during the Rope Course or Zipline activity.

People under the influence of alcohol or drugs will not be permitted to use the attraction.

The operator reserves the right to expel participants from the attraction if they display disruptive or dangerous behaviour. The operator's decision is final.

Rope Course participation is at your own risk.

Pregnant women and guests with high blood pressure, heart conditions, recent surgery or illness, back, neck or bone injuries, or equilibrium/ear problems should not use the attraction.

Only one participant is permitted to stand in the tower together with the operator at any time.

Do not use any rope trail with loose hair, loose clothing or objects that could become caught in the rope or safety equipment.
